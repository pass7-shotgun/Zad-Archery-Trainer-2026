## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Zad Archery**. This trainer enhances Zad Archery's progression loop by providing optional controls for combat performance, currencies, skill development, equipment upgrades, crafting materials, and wave progression. It is designed for the single-player Windows release and lets players customize the pace and difficulty of their archer's development.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with skill-tree builds, Archer Jobs, crafting, alchemy, loot, and wave progression without repeating the normal resource grind.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Zad Archery is built around a stationary 2D combat lane rather than free-roaming exploration. The archer remains positioned on the left side of the scene while target-like monsters approach from the right, so the trainer focuses on combat and progression instead of movement controls.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the trainer's resource and equipment options after collecting loot or opening the relevant upgrade screens. Unlimited materials can support bow and armor crafting, while progression resources make it easier to test different equipment and skill-tree choices. Disable resource options before saving if you want the resulting inventory to remain closer to normal values.

### World & Environment

The game progresses through combat waves on a 2D hillside battlefield. Enemies arrive in successive groups, and clearing waves produces loot and resources used to strengthen the archer. Wave controls are useful for testing late-game builds or moving through familiar content more quickly.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on shooting waves of target-like monsters with an archer. Activate enhanced damage or rapid attacks to increase clearing speed, and use infinite health when testing difficult encounters. Instant wave clear is best reserved for skipping completed content or verifying progression settings.
